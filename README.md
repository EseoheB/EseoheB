## 🚀 Featured projects

### [Cloud Resource Management and Cost Optimization System](https://github.com/EseoheB/cloud-cost-optimization-db)
A 19-table MySQL database that tracks cloud usage, budgets, department chargeback, and invoice matching. I tested indexes on 2 million synthetic usage events with EXPLAIN ANALYZE and cut a per-resource cost query from 1.5 s to 23 ms. Results feed a [live Tableau dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/rabiat.blessing.ibrahim/viz/CloudCostOptimizationDashboard_17918460822610/CloudCostOptimization).
`MySQL` `SQL` `Tableau` `query tuning`

### [Understanding Magazine Subscription Behavior](https://github.com/EseoheB/marketing-campaign-subscription-prediction)
Predicted which customers will subscribe using logistic regression and SVM, when only 14.9% of customers said yes. The SVM reached 86.70% AUC, and past campaign response, recent purchases, and customer tenure were the strongest drivers.
`Python` `scikit-learn` `pandas` `classification`

### [MLOps Pipeline](https://github.com/EseoheB/mlops-pipeline)
An end-to-end pipeline that sorts documents from the 20 Newsgroups dataset using TF-IDF and logistic regression, reaching 90.56% accuracy. It covers preprocessing, training, and evaluation, and serves the model through a Flask API with `/health` and `/predict` endpoints.
`Python` `NLP` `Flask` `MLOps`

### [Climate Emission Strategy Analysis](https://github.com/EseoheB/climate-emission-strategy-analysis)
Analyzed 2.4 million emission reduction records from Climate TRACE and ranked strategies two ways: by raw impact, and by impact adjusted for how hard they are to carry out. The analysis points out sectors where the two rankings disagree and highlights the more practical choice.
`Python` `data analysis` `climate`

### [HIV Trends in Sub-Saharan Africa](https://github.com/EseoheB/sub-saharan-hiv-analysis)
A MySQL database that combines UNAIDS HIV estimates with World Bank economic data for 49 countries from 2007 to 2024, with R scripts and a [live Tableau dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/rabiat.blessing.ibrahim/viz/Sub-SaharanAfricaHIVTrends2007-2024/HIVTrendsinSub-SaharanAfrica). Finding and fixing 116 values that understated new infections and deaths revealed a 60% drop in new infections across the region.
`MySQL` `R` `Tableau` `public health`

### [NYC Taxi BigQuery Analysis](https://github.com/EseoheB/nyc-taxi-bigquery-analysis)
SQL analysis of 146 million NYC yellow taxi trips in Google BigQuery, built with a three-person team. My queries compare card and cash riders by hour and measure revenue per mile. After fixing an outlier problem, they showed that hours with longer trips earn less per mile (r = -0.90).
`BigQuery` `SQL` `big data`
